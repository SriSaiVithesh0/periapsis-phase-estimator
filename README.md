
# Periapsis Phase Estimator

### Conditioning of periapsis-shift extraction from orbit integrations: an accumulated-phase estimator and its error scaling

**Author:** Uppala Sri Sai Vithesh  
**Affiliation:** Independent Student Researcher, Andhra Pradesh, India  
**Email:** srisaivitheshuppala99@gmail.com

## About the Project

This research investigates a numerical method for extracting periapsis shifts from orbital integrations.

It develops and evaluates an accumulated-phase estimator and compares its numerical performance with the conventional event-based approach.

The primary investigation uses Schwarzschild orbital dynamics, with an additional mathematical test system to examine the estimator's behaviour.

## Research Objectives

- Develop an accumulated-phase approach to periapsis-shift extraction.
- Derive its mathematical formulation.
- Compare it with conventional event-based estimation.
- Investigate numerical accuracy and error scaling.
- Examine performance across different orbital regimes and computational protocols.

## Main Findings

The numerical experiments indicate that the phase-based estimator can provide substantial accuracy advantages in the tested weak-field configurations.

However, its performance is not universally superior. Results in intermediate and strong-field regimes depend on the computational protocol.

The research also compares the approach with alternative numerical methods, including quadrature-based calculations.

## Scope and Limitations

This work is a numerical-method investigation, not a new theory of gravity.

The current study focuses primarily on planar Schwarzschild orbital dynamics and a supplementary mathematical test system. Its conclusions are limited to the models, solvers, and configurations investigated.

Further work is needed to examine more general dynamical systems and independent computational implementations.
